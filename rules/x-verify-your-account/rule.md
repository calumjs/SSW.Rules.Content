As of 21 November 2025, X ties verification to paid subscriptions. Since April 2023, blue checks have only gone to accounts that keep an active X Premium (individual) or Premium Business (organization) plan, pass a light eligibility review, and remain in good standing.

<!--endintro-->

## Current verification paths (2025)

### Individuals – Subscribe to X Premium

1. **Pick a tier:** Basic ($3/month on web, $32/year) unlocks long posts and editing but **does not** include a badge. Premium ($8/month, $84/year) and Premium+ ($40/month, $420/year) both deliver the blue check plus the feature bundle—choose the one that fits your needs and budget.
2. **Complete the eligibility checklist:** Before X’s automated review runs, confirm your profile is public, has a display name and photo, and a confirmed phone number. Accounts with recent policy violations, impersonation flags, or no activity will fail review. Changing your @handle, name, or avatar later temporarily removes the badge until X re-runs eligibility.
3. **Let the automated review finish:** Once subscribed, X runs an automated verification check that can take a few business days. The blue check appears automatically once you pass and remains visible only while the subscription stays active.
4. **Use organization affiliations when available:** Individuals invited as affiliates of a Premium Business (Verified Organizations) account inherit verification without buying their own subscription, but they lose both the check and affiliate badge immediately if the organization revokes the link.

### Organizations – Enrol in Premium Business (Verified Organizations)

* **Choose Basic or Full Access:** Premium Business Basic costs $200/month ($2,000/year) for the gold check, square avatar, and priority support. Full Access costs $1,000/month ($10,000/year) and layers on affiliate management, impersonation defense, and custom brand controls. Prices can vary by country, so confirm your local rate before submitting payment.
* **Complete business verification:** During onboarding you submit corporate documents, official email domains, and billing info so X can validate authenticity. Once approved, your main handle displays the gold check automatically.
* **Manage affiliates:** Full Access customers can invite people, departments, or partner accounts to display an affiliate badge. Each accepted affiliate inherits Premium-level verification and the organization’s logo badge; removing the affiliation immediately removes both.
* **Keep subscriptions active:** Letting the organization’s subscription lapse removes the gold check, affiliate badges, and any inherited verification from linked accounts, so budget for ongoing renewal.

## Legacy verification checklist (pre-April 2023)

X (then Twitter) used to run a manual application process. Applicants had to prove they were **authentic, notable, and active** before Trust & Safety staff granted a badge. Typical requirements included:

* Submitting ID, an official email, or an official website link to prove identity.
* Demonstrating notability in categories such as Government, Companies/Brands, News, Entertainment, Sports & Gaming, and Activists/Influential Individuals, often by providing recent press coverage, Wikipedia entries, or evidence of large follower counts.
* Maintaining an active, public profile with a name, profile image, and a confirmed email or phone number, plus no serious policy violations in the prior 12 months.

X began winding down this legacy workflow on 1 April 2023. Keep these historic notes only if you need to understand how older badges were awarded—for any new verification, follow the paid subscription model above.
