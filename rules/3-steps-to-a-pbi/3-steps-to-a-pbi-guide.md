# Three Steps to a Product Backlog Item (PBI)

## Introduction
A Product Backlog Item (PBI) is a crucial component in Agile methodologies, representing a single unit of work that adds value to the product. Properly creating and managing PBIs ensures that teams can prioritize work effectively.

## Steps to Create a PBI
1. **Identify the Need**: Understand the requirement or user story that necessitates a new backlog item.
2. **Define the Acceptance Criteria**: Create clear criteria that must be met for the item to be considered complete, ensuring that the team and stakeholders have a mutual understanding of the expectations.
3. **Estimate Effort**: As a team, assess the effort required to complete the PBI, using suitable estimation techniques relevant to your process (e.g., Planning Poker).
